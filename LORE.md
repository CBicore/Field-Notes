# Field Notes Lore

> Every person leaves a trail. Most trails disappear. These notes preserve what remains.

## The Archive

The Field Notes belong to an unfinished archive of people who changed their worlds—sometimes loudly, sometimes without anyone noticing.

No one knows who began the archive.

Its pages have passed between travelers, investigators, historians, storytellers, and strangers who felt compelled to write something down before it was forgotten. Each keeper added what they witnessed, what they were told, and what they suspected.

The archive does not distinguish heroes from villains. It records them all.

## The Rules of the Notes

Every Field Note should answer three questions:

1. **Who does this person appear to be?**
2. **What are they hiding?**
3. **What might they become?**

Facts are recorded plainly. Rumors are marked. Contradictions are never erased.

A crossed-out sentence may be more truthful than the sentence that replaced it.

## The Marks

Certain symbols appear throughout the archive:

- **The Eye** — Something witnessed directly.
- **The Flame** — A desire powerful enough to cause change.
- **The Thorn** — A wound that still shapes the character.
- **The Thread** — A relationship connecting two lives.
- **The Door** — A choice that cannot be undone.
- **The Star** — A possibility not yet fulfilled.
- **The Hollow Circle** — Missing information or a deliberate silence.

## The Unwritten

Some pages contain blank spaces where names should be.

Some describe meetings that have not happened yet.

Occasionally, a new note appears in handwriting none of the keepers recognize. These entries are always brief, strangely accurate, and signed with the same symbol:

**A compass pointing nowhere.**

## The Keeper’s Oath

> I will record what is known.  
> I will preserve what is uncertain.  
> I will not mistake rumor for truth,  
> nor truth for the whole story.  
> Every life contains a hidden country.  
> These notes are only its map.

## A Living Archive

The Field Notes are never truly complete.

Characters change. Memories distort. Secrets surface. Old enemies become allies, and trusted friends reveal unfamiliar faces.

Every new detail alters the map.

The archive grows with them.
