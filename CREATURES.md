# Field Notes: Creatures of the Gentle Wilds

## Pippin the Mycora

**Kind:** Leaf-Mycora  
**Role:** Guardian of seedlings and lost travelers  
**Gift:** Verdant sparks that encourage growth and soothe distress

Pippin is a patient mushroom-folk wanderer with a bright scarlet cap and a body shaped from leaves, roots, and living earth. They travel the quietest forest paths, waking dormant seeds and leaving small signs for anyone who has lost their way.

Pippin rarely speaks above a whisper. They believe frightened creatures should be approached slowly and that every living thing grows best when it feels safe. Although dependable when guiding others, Pippin secretly fears traveling beyond the forest they know. Their greatest journey may begin when the path home suddenly disappears.

## Brightwing

**Kind:** Glimmerdrake Spriggle  
**Role:** Keeper of the Dawnstone  
**Gift:** Gathering morning light and revealing safe paths

Brightwing is a young forest dragon with sunflower-gold frills, bright amber eyes, and more courage than caution. They carry a glowing Dawnstone everywhere, convinced its light was entrusted to them for an important reason.

Cheerful, loyal, and endlessly curious, Brightwing loves leading travelers through shadowed places. Unfortunately, they sometimes race ahead before checking whether anyone is following. Brightwing’s deepest fear is not darkness but discovering that the Dawnstone never needed a guardian. Their story begins when the stone dims—and the smallest dragon in the forest must learn that hope can shine without it.

## Glowmorrow

**Kind:** Dusk-born light spirit  
**Role:** Hearth guardian and keeper of creative spaces  
**Gift:** Turning happiness into light and music

Glowmorrow awakens in the breath between daylight and night. Like others of its kind, it was born within a humming hive called a murmuration. Its warm glow deepens around laughter, art, music, and rooms that have been truly loved.

This particular Glowmorrow is unusually independent. Rather than remain with its murmuration, it searches for lonely homes that have forgotten how to make their own light. It communicates through soft melodic vibrations, with every emotion producing a different song. Its greatest weakness is silence: in places emptied of imagination and affection, its glow begins to fade.

## Pupkin

**Kind:** Pastel guardian familiar  
**Role:** Protector of rest, tenderness, and emotional safety  
**Gift:** Calming distressed hearts and softening troubled spaces

Pupkin is a gentle, dog-like spirit colored in rose, lavender, peach, and sky blue. Its tufted tail glows whenever someone nearby needs comfort, even when that person insists they are fine.

Quietly observant and fiercely affectionate, Pupkin never forces companionship. It simply stays close until fear loosens its grip. Pupkin can absorb the sharp edges of sorrow, but carrying too much sadness causes its colors to fade. Its journey is about learning that a guardian also deserves care—and that gentleness becomes stronger when it is shared.

## Froggicorn

**Kind:** Horned pond spirit  
**Role:** Guide at the boundary between the ordinary and enchanted  
**Gift:** Revealing possibilities hidden beneath still surfaces

Froggicorn appears only where imagination and inner calm exist together. Its golden-speckled skin resembles moonlight scattered across pond water, while its shimmering horn detects wishes that have never been spoken aloud.

Playful but perceptive, Froggicorn encourages hesitant dreamers to make their first brave leap. It dislikes being treated as a grand prophetic creature and often delivers wisdom immediately before falling off a lily pad. Beneath its humor lies a private uncertainty: Froggicorn can reveal doors for everyone except itself. One evening, the pond shows it a future it is afraid to enter.

## Pearl, the Mermaid Bear

**Kind:** Tidewood bear  
**Role:** Guardian of the meeting place between sea and forest  
**Gift:** Moving safely through saltwater, freshwater, and woodland magic

Pearl was born where the tide reaches the roots of ancient trees. She has iridescent scales, soft pastel fur, and ears adorned with pearls collected from songs the ocean has forgotten.

Warmhearted and quietly formidable, Pearl protects creatures displaced by storms and guides them toward new homes. She treasures objects washed ashore because she believes nothing carried by the sea is truly lost. Pearl’s conflict begins when the ocean returns something that belonged to her long ago—something she had finally learned to live without.

## Blue Fairy Nestling

**Kind:** Dew-pearl fairy familiar  
**Role:** Keeper of sanctuary and creative clarity  
**Gift:** Settling restless thoughts and restoring gentle focus

The Blue Fairy Nestling is a tiny, round familiar covered in soft blue plumage. It hatched from a dew-pearl left beneath moonlit leaves and now seeks spaces that need peace more than excitement.

The Nestling is calm, attentive, and remarkably stubborn about bedtime. It collects unfinished ideas, holding them safely until their creators are ready to return. Although its magic makes other creatures feel at home, the Nestling has never chosen a permanent home of its own. Its story changes when it finds a sanctuary worth protecting—and realizes that staying requires a different kind of courage than wandering.

## Shared World Thread

Together, these creatures inhabit **the Gentle Wilds**, a living borderland where emotional needs take magical form.

Pippin nurtures growth. Brightwing carries hope. Glowmorrow protects joy. Pupkin offers comfort. Froggicorn awakens courage. Pearl helps the displaced find belonging. The Blue Fairy Nestling creates sanctuary.

Their stories can stand alone, but they are also connected by an approaching event: paths are vanishing, Dawnstones are dimming, murmurations have stopped singing, and the border between the ordinary world and the Gentle Wilds is beginning to close.
